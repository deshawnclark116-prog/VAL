Import draw history

CSV columns: game,at,stream,numbers,bonus. Use an ISO timestamp with a timezone, quote space-separated numbers, and leave bonus blank for games without a selected special ball. Digit games require Day or Night. Keno rows contain all 20 winning numbers.

Imports are validated before writing. Existing draw identifiers are retained, avoiding accidental replacement.

What the math can tell us

How numbers appeared in the past. Whether a method performed differently from a random control in a recorded test. How much variation chance alone can create.

What it cannot promise

A reliable edge on a fair independent draw. A backtest or a few wins is not proof of future performance. Use paper picks while evaluating the bot.
